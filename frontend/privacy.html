Privacy notice

Plain-language summary of how AURAQ handles data. Last updated: October 2026.

Who runs this

AURAQ is run by Rudraksh Tiwari as an independent project. Questions or deletion requests: YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.

What we collect

  • Shop owners: name, email, a scrambled (hashed) password, and the business details, products and FAQs you enter.
  • Customers who chat with a shop's assistant on Telegram: your Telegram name, username and chat ID, the messages you send, and, if you place an order request, the name and contact details you give.

Why

To let the assistant answer customers and let the shop owner read chats, review order requests and confirm or reject them.

Who can see it

  • The shop owner whose link you used can read your chat and your order details.
  • Messages are sent to an AI provider (currently Google's Gemini API) to write replies. We may change provider in future.
  • Our hosting and database providers store the data for us.
  • We do not sell your data.

The assistant is an AI

Replies are written by an AI and can be wrong. Orders are only requests until the shop owner confirms them. Do not send passwords, card numbers or other sensitive information in the chat.

How long we keep it

Until the shop owner deletes it or you ask us to. Email us to have your data removed.

Security

Passwords are stored only as hashes, each owner can only access their own shop's data, and connections use HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your login private.

Changes

We may update this notice and will change the date above when we do.
